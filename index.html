Research

H I Survey · FAST

FAST–M31

Investigating background H I sources in the M31 halo region using FAST. My work includes source catalog construction, validation, and estimation of the H I mass function.

Deep H I Survey · FAST

HD² Survey

Contributing to the FAST Hundred-deg² H I Deep Survey through observation planning, observing strategies, HiFAST data reduction, and H I source extraction.

HD² Survey Website ↗
Galaxy Evolution · FEASTS

FEASTS

Investigating how environments affect the H I content of satellite galaxies, including gas depletion, stripping, and the evolution of diffuse gas around nearby systems.
