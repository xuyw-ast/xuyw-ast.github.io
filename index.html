About

Neutral hydrogen and galaxy evolution

I am a Ph.D. student at the National Astronomical Observatories, Chinese Academy of Sciences. My research focuses on how neutral hydrogen traces the gas content and evolution of nearby galaxies.

In the FAST–M31 survey, I am building a catalog of background H I sources and studying the H I mass function. With FEASTS, I investigate the H I properties of satellite galaxies and their environments.

Institution: National Astronomical Observatories, Chinese Academy of Sciences
Research Interests: Neutral Hydrogen (H I), Galaxy Evolution, Radio Astronomy
Current Projects: FAST–M31, FEASTS, H I survey science
